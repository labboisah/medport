# MediPass Product Overview

MediPass | Working specification | October 2026

## Vision

Make relevant medical history available to the right care team at the right time, with patient control, clear provenance, and appropriate safeguards.

## Problem statement

Patients often attend different hospitals or clinics, and prior diagnoses, prescriptions, laboratory results, allergies, or discharge notes may not follow them. This can cause repeated questions, delays, duplicate investigations, and incomplete clinical context.

## Illustrative use case

A patient is assessed for malaria at one hospital, receives a diagnosis and medication, then travels and visits another hospital because symptoms recur. With permission, the second clinician can review the earlier diagnosis, medicines, and results instead of relying only on memory or starting from zero.

## Target users

Patients and caregivers; clinicians; hospitals and clinics; diagnostic laboratories; pharmacies; specialists; facility administrators; integration partners.

## Product principles

Patient-centered access; minimum necessary disclosure; interoperability; source transparency; secure-by-default sharing; support for facilities with and without EHRs; accessibility and low-bandwidth usability.

## Scope for first release

Patient identity and matching; patient record timeline; document and structured-result upload; consent and share links; provider portal; facility onboarding; audit trail; basic referral workflow; Mediflow integration proof of concept.

## Out of scope for initial release

Replacing hospital information systems; autonomous diagnosis or treatment recommendations; unrestricted national record search; unreviewed AI-generated clinical conclusions.

## Success measures

Successful patient matching; time to retrieve authorized history; percentage of records with clear source and date; provider adoption; consent comprehension; low rate of incorrect record linkage; incident response readiness.
