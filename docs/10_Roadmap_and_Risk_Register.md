# Roadmap and Risk Register

MediPass | Working specification | October 2026

## Phase 1: Discovery

Interview patients and care providers; confirm clinical workflows; validate legal/privacy requirements; select pilot facilities; agree data-sharing terms; define minimum clinical dataset and matching rules.

## Phase 2: Foundation

Build identity, facility registry, provider verification, patient timeline, consent and access controls, document handling, audit, and secure provider portal.

## Phase 3: Pilot integrations

Integrate Mediflow and one or more willing external facilities; test source attribution, matching, synchronization, consent, and incident response using synthetic and approved pilot data.

## Phase 4: Controlled pilot

Train users, measure workflow performance, collect patient and clinician feedback, review audit events, resolve safety issues, and establish support procedures.

## Phase 5: Expansion

Add facilities and integration adapters, improve referrals, reporting, low-bandwidth support, and carefully governed emergency workflows based on pilot evidence.

## Key risks

Incorrect patient matching; unauthorized access; low provider adoption; incomplete or stale records; inconsistent data formats; integration costs; connectivity limitations; unclear consent; operational support burden; regulatory non-compliance.

## Mitigations

Human review for uncertain matches; least privilege and audits; clear facility value proposition; provenance and freshness labels; standards plus adapters; phased integration; explicit consent UX; local legal review; training and incident drills.

## Go/no-go criteria

Proceed only when pilot users can reliably match patients, consent boundaries are enforced, access events are auditable, source information is visible, critical security issues are closed, and support and recovery procedures have been tested.
